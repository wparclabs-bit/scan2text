## Locked decisions
- D-01 — the Cloud engine speaks the OpenAI-compatible chat-completions shape only.
- D-15 — a band is five consecutive pages in one request; the size is a Profile setting.
- D-17 — the prompt asks for HTML tables; the existing HTML-to-GFM converter produces the final Markdown.
- D-27 — tables with 13 or more columns are rebuilt from text geometry, not from the model.
- D-37 — the spike records base64 acceptance, token counts for a 150 DPI page and a 300 DPI table crop at five per band, latency, and the cost of a 30-page document.
- D-38 — pass thresholds: at most $0.10 per 30-page document, band latency at most 20 seconds, at least 98% table-cell accuracy, zero malformed tables, zero hallucinated columns.

## Measured record — 2026-11-15

Environment and recipe.
- Endpoint: `https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1/chat/completions`, OpenAI-compatible chat completions.
- Model: `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b`, chosen because it accepted base64 images, answered in seconds, and follows the chat shape; the alternatives are recorded under Handoff notes.
- Recipe: temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95, `chat_template_kwargs {"enable_thinking": false}`. Reasoning was off in every scored call (`reasoning_tokens` 0), matching D-04.
- Prompt: transcribe exactly, preserve reading order, emit tables as HTML `<table>` (D-17). No commentary, no code fences.
- Inputs: synthetic pages drawn with Pillow 12.3.0 on Python 3.12; pages A4 at 150 DPI (1240 by 1754 px); table crops 6 columns by 20 rows at 300 DPI; the wide fixture 45 columns by 3 rows at 300 DPI. Ground truth was generated with the images, so cell matching is exact string matching after case and whitespace normalisation.
- Cost basis: the model's page lists serverless rates of $0.15 to $0.20 per 1M input tokens and $0.47 to $0.50 per 1M output tokens. The highest pair, $0.20 input and $0.50 output, is used below. Prompt caching was observed on repeat bands (8672 cached of 10835 prompt tokens for pages, 4336 of 8386 for crops) but is ignored, so the cost figures are conservative.
- The figures come from one session. Latency varies with provider load; repeat bands are shown to give a range.

Derived per unit: a 150 DPI page inside a five-page band costs 2167 prompt and 381 completion tokens; a 300 DPI table crop inside a five-crop band costs 1677 prompt and 1576 completion tokens. Token counts were identical across repeats, so the figures are not averaged.

Table quality, scored cell by cell against the generated grid.

| Fixture | Cells | Matched | Accuracy | Columns returned / expected | Hallucinated columns | Malformed tables |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6x20 crop, one request | 126 | 126 | 100.0% | 6 / 6 | 0 | 0 |
| 6x20 crops inside three band requests (15 tables) | 1890 | 1890 | 100.0% | 6 / 6 | 0 | 0 of 15 |
| 45x3 crop, one request | 180 | 45 | 25.0% | 45 / 45 | 0 | 0 |

The wide-fixture failure is a legibility failure, not a structure failure: the model returned exactly 45 columns with the headers `C01` to `C45` and no invented column, but every data cell lost its trailing digits (`C01R01` came back as `C01R`). At 300 DPI a 45-column row leaves roughly 53 pixels per column. This is the regime D-27 predicts and routes to text geometry.

|---|---|---|---|
| Full pages at 150 DPI | 65010 | 11418 | $0.0187 |
| Table crops at 300 DPI | 50316 | 47286 | $0.0337 |

Threshold verdicts.

| D-38 threshold | Verdict | Evidence and consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Hosted endpoint accepts base64 images (D-37) | PASSED | HTTP 200 for one image and for five images in one request. |
| Cost at or below $0.10 per 30-page document | PASSED | $0.019 on the full-page path, $0.034 on the table-crop path, at the highest published serverless rate. |
| Band latency at or below 20 seconds | FAILED | A five-page 150 DPI band is 12.7 to 14.1 s and passes. A five-crop 300 DPI table band is 53.2 to 78.6 s and fails; the completion alone is 7881 tokens because the model retypes every table. Plan change: five-page bands stay, but five 300 DPI table crops must not be one model request. Narrow tables ride along with the page band; wide tables stay on the D-27 geometry path. |
| At least 98% table-cell accuracy | FAILED | 6-column crops score 100% (126 of 126, and 1890 of 1890 across three bands). The 45-column crop scores 25% (45 of 180). Plan change: the 98% threshold cannot gate a direct 45-column model request; it must be scoped to the geometry path for 13-or-more-column tables (D-27) and to model crops of 12 columns or fewer. |
| Zero malformed tables | PASSED | 0 of 17 scored tables were malformed: one `<table>` each, uniform row widths, no empty structure. |
| Zero hallucinated columns on the 45-column fixture | PASSED | 45 of 45 headers returned verbatim, 0 invented columns, even though cell contents were lost. |

- The next slice may assume: the hosted endpoint accepts base64 PNG images, one or five per message; the model `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-super-vl-120b-a12b` answers the recipe temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95, `chat_template_kwargs {"enable_thinking": false}` with reasoning off; token counts are stable across repeats (deterministic per image set).

- What the failed thresholds change in the plan:
  - Five 300 DPI table crops must not be sent as one model request; the measured band is 53 to 79 seconds. Keep five-page bands for pages, let narrow tables ride inside the page band, and keep wide tables on the D-27 geometry path.
  - The 98% cell-accuracy threshold must be scoped: it gates model crops of 12 columns or fewer (measured 100%) and the geometry path for 13-or-more-column tables. A direct 45-column model crop measured 25% and cannot be the gate.
  - D-15 and D-38 therefore need a one-line amendment by the owner. This slice records the recommendation and does not edit `docs/slices/DECISIONS.md`.

- Privacy finding that affects copy: the model page states that trial input and output are recorded and warns against uploading confidential information. The consent dialog wording (D-12) and the open licence item (D-54) must reflect that.
- Still open: the cost is a list-price estimate from the published serverless rates, not an invoice; baseline figures held at 710 frontend and 447 backend tests; no continuous integration exists yet, so these gates ran locally.
